# IITD Research Ambit
*A student-led initiative to make IIT Delhi’s research accessible, searchable, and impactful.*

---

## 🚀 About the Organisation

**IITD Research Ambit** is an open, student-driven research intelligence platform built at the **Indian Institute of Technology Delhi**.  
Our mission is to **bring every research contribution from IITD — across departments, centres, labs, faculty, and research groups — into one unified, searchable hub**.

World-class research at IITD spans engineering, sciences, humanities, technology, entrepreneurship, and interdisciplinary domains. But this knowledge remains fragmented across lab websites, PDFs, newsletters, and isolated systems.

**Research Ambit changes this.**  
We index, structure, and surface IITD’s research outputs so that **students, faculty, industry partners, and collaborators** can access them effortlessly.

---

## 🎯 Vision

- Create the **single source of truth** for IITD research.  
- Simplify discovery using **full-text and semantic search**.  
- Build a platform where **ideas, innovations, and researchers become discoverable instantly**.  
- Showcase IITD’s impactful research to the **global academic and industry community**.

---

## 🧩 What We Are Building

### 🔍 1. Global Research Search Engine
A fast, intuitive search experience with:
- Keyword + semantic search  
- Filters for department, faculty, year, domain, publication type  
- Cross-paper contextual understanding  

### 📚 2. IITD Research Corpus
A unified, structured repository of:
- Publications  
- Patents  
- Research projects  
- Preprints  
- Lab updates  
- Student theses  
- Media mentions  

Backed by automated pipelines to **ingest, clean, and index data**.

### 🧠 3. AI-Powered Research Navigator
NLP/LLM-powered modules to:
- Summarize research  
- Extract methods, datasets, keywords  
- Provide non-expert explanations  
- Generate impact briefs for industry & public  

### 🎨 4. Public-Facing Portal
A modern, accessible web interface offering:
- Live research search  
- Researcher profiles  
- Trending topics  
- Lab-wise pages  
- Collaboration opportunities  

### 🛠️ 5. Modular Architecture
Our repositories include:
- `data-pipelines` → scrapers, normalizers  
- `search-index` → keyword + vector indexes  
- `api` → unified research API  
- `frontend` → web UI  
- `models` → NLP/LLM tasks
